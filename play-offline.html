How to play

  1. Pick a seed from your seed pouch (beside the board, or below it on a phone).
  2. Tap an empty square on the board to plant it. Tap it again to pick it back up.
  3. Press "Let dusk fall" and watch what your plants do in the dark.

Goal: make every visitor happy

Each plant follows a secret rule. Nobody tells you the rules: working them out is the game. You can't lose, and you can try as many times as you like. Stuck? Ask the owl.

About Deepvan

A calm puzzle game about the secret logic of nature.

Play at your own pace. There are no timers, no lost lives, and nothing you do here can't be undone.

How it works

  1. Listen to the visitors. Moths want warm light, toads want shade, snails wait for their Shadefern to flower, and beetles hope for moss by morning.
  2. Plant and watch. Choose seeds from your pouch and press "Let dusk fall". Watch how light travels and moss grows in the dark.
  3. Work out the rules. Nobody tells you what each plant does. Finding out by trying things is the heart of the puzzle.

The science underneath

As you guide travellers along the Lantern Road, you aren't only gardening: you are quietly building circuits, logic gates and living patterns. Finish a chapter to see the real science behind what you made, in your Journal.
