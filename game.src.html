How to play

  1. Pick a seed from your seed pouch (beside the board, or below it on a phone).
  2. Tap an empty square on the board to plant it. Tap it again to pick it back up.
  3. Press "Let dusk fall" and watch what your plants do in the dark.

Goal: make every visitor happy

Each plant follows a secret rule. Nobody tells you the rules: working them out is the game. You can't lose, and you can try as many times as you like. Stuck? Ask the owl.
